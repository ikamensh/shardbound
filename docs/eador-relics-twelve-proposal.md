# Four further relic choices

Accepted direction, 2026-09-06; now implemented after the v11 roster, with
[earned journeys and explicit balance limits](eador-active-relics.md).
The extension reaches twelve without another combat subsystem or stat bonuses.
One equipped slot remains the constraint. Ordinary keep/sell/duplicate choices
remain; 45 gold is an initial sale-price hypothesis.

| Relic | Source in newly generated worlds | Decision and counter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Veil Censer | Courier's Crossing, replacing its duplicate Merchant Seal. Lost Caravan retains the Seal. | One Smoke charge, with the Sapper's exact rules. Spends the hero's order and blocks friendly ranged spells too; melee and moving clear counter it. |
| Porter's Rune | Supply Cache, replacing its duplicate Oak Standard. Elder Grove retains Oak. | One Repulse charge, with the Rune Adept's exact rules. Adjacent only; Guard/Brace anchors and occupied/edge landings prevent displacement. A hero who Repulses cannot also evacuate that turn. |
| Mirror Badge | The planned Sealed Vault on Ruins' western flank, (-1, 1), replacing its duplicate Iron Crown after the roster is stable. Old Barrow retains the Crown. | Existing Swap. The hero takes the exposed position and spends its order; both units spend movement. The rescued ally keeps only its already-unspent action. |
| Vanguard Drum | A new Muster Yard on Frontier's western flank, proposed (-1, 1), with Archer/Brigand/Brigand guards. | Existing Militia Rally. Trades the hero's attack or Heal for one adjacent ally's mobility. Removes Pin only; no healing, cargo removal, cooldown refund or refreshed action. |

A flight relic is deliberately omitted: it would risk replacing Wayfarer
Boots' role and bypassing the extraction carrier's movement tradeoff.
These four orders compete directly with the current damage, recovery,
economy and terrain equipment. Source names alone do not count as completed
authored adventure patterns under G05.

## Public acceptance journeys

- Earn Veil Censer at the actual Crossing. In a later Watch battle, compare
  Smoke with Guard from the same save: less incoming ranged harm, blocked
  friendly spells, cloud expiry and exact continuation. Complete a real hold
  with defenders alive.
- Earn Porter's Rune at Cache and carry it to Last Gate. Displace a seal
  contester without killing it, then complete the hold. Paired anchored and
  blocked-landing saves must reject unchanged. An exit case must wait for
  another hero order or use a troop Adept instead.
- Win Sealed Vault with an ordinary purchased army. At Gate, use Mirror Badge
  to extract a wounded holder while preserving its unspent attack. Save/reload
  and finish the hold. Show the explicit refusal to evacuate immediately
  after the hero's own Swap.
- Earn Vanguard Drum and face a real enemy Pin at Watch or Gate. Rally an
  unspent flank troop onto an otherwise unreachable objective route. Already
  moved or acted allies must gain no extra order.

Each source must be discoverable before travel and generated correctly across
100 seeds of its theme. Preserve home Shrine, Watch, Den and Explorer's Camp.
Every saved province reward, inventory, active capability tuple and linked
checkpoint retains its recorded meaning. Human discovery and balance review
remain necessary after these mechanical journeys pass.

The distinct combat-ability count excludes ordinary Guard, movement, attacks,
wait and evacuation. Bolt, Heal, Brace, Pin and Swap are five; the proposed
Smoke, Repulse and Militia Rally reach eight. Rally clears an adjacent living
ally's Pin while spending the Militia's movement/action, preserving the ally's
movement/action/retaliation/stance/cooldown/cargo fields exactly. Its manual
acceptance includes a saved enemy Pin, Rally and a restored route, plus spent
ally cases proving that it never grants an extra action.
